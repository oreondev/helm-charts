Reminders
  - NetworkPolicy only works if your CNI enforces it (Calico, Cilium, GKE Dataplane V2, AKS NPM/Cilium, EKS VPC CNI with network policy enabled).
  - Namespaces created here carry `helm.sh/resource-policy: keep`; `helm uninstall` leaves them (and their workloads) in place.
  - To manage a namespace that already exists, let Helm adopt it first:
      kubectl label ns <name> app.kubernetes.io/managed-by=Helm
      kubectl annotate ns <name> meta.helm.sh/release-name={{ .Release.Name }} meta.helm.sh/release-namespace={{ .Release.Namespace }}
